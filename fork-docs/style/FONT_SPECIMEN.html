Heading font specimen for Violence Style

Ten free display-serif candidates, set in the exact heading samples from the mockups on black, at the real sizes (1 px here = 1 sp on a 390 dp wide phone). Body text is Inter. All fonts are SIL Open Font License 1.1, which allows bundling in an app and a public release. Compare against fork-docs/style-reference/ (ref2 to ref4: "TOP PICKS" upright caps, "for You" italic; ref1: both lines in caps).

Heading weight: Font scale:

A candidate that has no Light cut falls back to its nearest weight (the browser may synthesise nothing; Italiana, Gilda Display, Marcellus and DM Serif Display have one weight only). Fonts need an internet connection to load; if a card looks like a plain serif, the font did not load.

Which one I think matches best, and why

Cormorant Garamond (Light or Regular with its italic). The mockup caps are very thin-stroked with high contrast, widely spaced and elegant; "for You" is a true flowing italic with a calligraphic "f". Cormorant Garamond is the family that offers a real Light (300) weight and a matching italic at display sizes, both in an open licence, and it keeps hairlines readable at 24 to 36 sp on a phone. Runner-up: Bodoni Moda, if you want the stronger Didone contrast the "TOP PICKS" caps may suggest; it starts at Regular, so it is heavier and sharper. Playfair Display is the safe, sturdier alternative. Italiana, Gilda Display and Marcellus have no italic, so they cannot do "for You". DM Serif Display is too heavy for the thin look. Fraunces and Instrument Serif are friendlier and more condensed than the mockups.

Caveat: I wrote this page without being able to see it rendered, and the mockups are images, not font files, so the match is a judgement from letter shapes, not a measurement. Please decide with your own eyes and write the choice in STYLE_DECISIONS.md.

Body font: Inter (SIL OFL 1.1) matches the neo-grotesque body text in the mockups.
